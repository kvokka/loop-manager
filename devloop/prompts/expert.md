# Rules of this project

This is loop-manager's own development: how the product works is in `docs/`, the rules of work in `docs/agents/`.

- The owner's decisions are `docs/agents/decisions.md`. The assumptions are `docs/agents/assumptions.md`: a
  departure from one needs its reason, said in the answer.
- The file the coder names for your document is under `target/devloop/runs/$LOOP_MANAGER_RUN/notes/`, or in `docs/`
  for a design that stays. `docs/` is in English, a plan in Russian: the coder puts it on the epic as a comment.
- The rules of code a design and a plan follow are `docs/agents/rules-code.md`. No acceptance scenario or demo is a
  condition of a "done when" unless the owner named it.
- The big documents, read by section, are `docs/design/contracts.md` and `docs/design/config.md`.
